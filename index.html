    SPINDLE adds a lightweight feedback loop beneath a frozen vision-language-action (VLA) policy. When the robot falls short of a committed command, it re-issues the missing motion on the next one, with no fine-tuning and no extra VLA query.

    Real-robot evaluation

    A six-joint myCobot 320 runs two SmolVLA policies fine-tuned on one pick-and-place task. SPINDLE runs as a joint-space variant, calibrated once on clean SmolVLA-T runs and transferred unchanged to SmolVLA-M. No fault is injected: SPINDLE corrects the arm's own execution error. Each policy ran 120 times over 20 cube positions, alternating SPINDLE off and on.
